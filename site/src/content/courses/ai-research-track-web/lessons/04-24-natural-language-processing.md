question: "Your GRU sentiment model gets 50% on IMDB while bag-of-words logistic regression gets 88%. The reviews are padded at the end to length 500. What is the likely bug?"
answer: "Post-padding without masking: the GRU reads hundreds of padding zeros after the review and its final state forgets the text. Use <code>mask_zero=True</code> on the embedding, or pre-padding, or take the state at each review&#39;s true last token."
