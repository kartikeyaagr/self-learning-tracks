That sounds easy until you count: 10 seconds at 30 frames per second is 300 images. Most of the ideas for video (sample a few frames, reuse an image network, add something that looks across time) are about keeping that cost under control. The same trade-offs appear with any sequence of high-dimensional inputs.</p>\n<h3>The idea in plain words</h3>\n<p>There are three common recipes, from cheapest to most expensive:</p>\n<ol>\n<li><strong>Frame features + averaging.</strong> Run a frozen image network (like Inception from 04.10) on a few frames, average their feature vectors, train a small classifier. Ignores order completely, but surprisingly strong.</li>\n<li><strong>Frame features + a sequence model.</strong> Feed the per-frame vectors, in order, to an LSTM or a small transformer, which can learn that &quot;hand moves up, then down&quot; differs from the reverse.</li>\n<li><strong>3D convolutions.</strong> Kernels that are, say, 3×3×3: they slide over height, width <em>and</em> time, so they can detect motion directly.</li>\n</ol>\n<h3>Worked example 1: the data size</h3>\n<p>A 10-second clip at 30 fps, frames resized to 224×224×3:</p>\n<ul>\n<li>One frame: <span class=\"katex\"><span class=\"katex-html\" aria-hidden=\"true\"><span class=\"katex-base\"><span class=\"katex-strut\" style=\"height:0.6444em;\"></span><span class=\"mord\">224</span><span class=\"mspace\" style=\"margin-right:0.2222em;\"></span><span class=\"mbin\">⋅</span><span class=\"mspace\" style=\"margin-right:0.2222em;\"></span></span><span class=\"katex-base\"><span class=\"katex-strut\" style=\"height:0.6444em;\"></span><span class=\"mord\">224</span><span class=\"mspace\" style=\"margin-right:0.2222em;\"></span><span class=\"mbin\">⋅</span><span class=\"mspace\" style=\"margin-right:0.2222em;\"></span></span><span class=\"katex-base\"><span class=\"katex-strut\" style=\"height:0.6444em;\"></span><span class=\"mord\">3</span><span class=\"mspace\" style=\"margin-right:0.2778em;\"></span><span class=\"mrel\">=</span><span class=\"mspace\" style=\"margin-right:0.2778em;\"></span></span><span class=\"katex-base\"><span class=\"katex-strut\" style=\"height:0.8389em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;\"></span><span class=\"mord\">150</span><span class=\"mord\"><span class=\"mpunct\">,</span></span><span class=\"mord\">528</span></span></span></span> numbers.</li>\n<li>300 frames: 45 M numbers, about 180 MB in float32.
