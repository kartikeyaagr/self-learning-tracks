question: "Training diverges in the first 200 steps at learning rate 3e-4 but is fine at 1e-4, which then trains too slowly. What schedule change would you try?"
answer: "Linear warm-up from near 0 to 3e-4 over the first few hundred to a few thousand steps, then cosine or linear decay. Early steps have large, noisy gradients and Adam&#39;s second-moment estimates are not yet reliable; warm-up avoids huge early updates."
