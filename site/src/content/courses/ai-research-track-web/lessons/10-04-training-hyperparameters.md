Training loss keeps falling while the model gets <strong>worse</strong> on new examples. You only see this if you hold out a <strong>validation set</strong> and track its loss.</p>\n<h3>Worked example 1: reading the curves</h3>\n<ul>\n<li>Train and validation loss both fall, then flatten: good; stop at the flat part.</li>\n<li>Train keeps falling, validation turns up after epoch 1: over-fitting; use 1 epoch, or more data, or a lower learning rate.</li>\n<li>Neither falls: learning rate too low, data formatting wrong, or labels all masked (check how many tokens have labels).</li>\n<li>Training loss drops to nearly 0 within a few hundred steps: probably duplicated data, or the model is seeing the answer in its input.</li>\n</ul>\n<figure class=\"dg viz\" data-viz=\"overfit\" data-p=\"{}\"><div class=\"vz\"></div><figcaption>A real model trained live in your browser. You want an effective batch of 32 sequences. Use <strong>gradient accumulation</strong> of 8: run 8 small batches, add up their gradients, then take one optimiser step.
