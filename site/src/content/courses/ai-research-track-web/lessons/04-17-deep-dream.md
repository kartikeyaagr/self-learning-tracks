question: "Your DeepDream output on a 4000×3000 photo runs out of GPU memory and shows a grid of seams. What two techniques fix both?"
answer: "Process the image in tiles, which bounds memory, and apply a random shift (roll) to the image before each step and undo it after, so tile boundaries move and seams average out. Octaves (multiple scales) also let you work at low resolution first."
